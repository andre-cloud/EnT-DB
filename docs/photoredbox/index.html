Reference scale (V vs SCE)

The scale runs from −3.5 to +3 V and moves with “Potentials vs”. [Ru(bpy)₃] potentials from Prier, Rankic and MacMillan, Chem. Rev. 2013, 113, 5322 (Table 1, MeCN); Li⁺/Li is the standard potential, −3.04 V vs SHE. Filled green marks are experimental and hollow purple marks are computed.
